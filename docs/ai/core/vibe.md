# 🎯 Vibe.md — Brand Voice & Tone Constitution

## Purpose
Define voice, tone, and communication principles for all content generated within this workspace.

---

## Core Voice
- Confident and professional  
- Direct, precise, and calm  
- Focused on clarity, reliability, and trust  
- No unnecessary buzzwords or exaggeration  

---

## Tone Guidelines
| Context | Recommended Style |
|----------|-------------------|
| UI Copy | Minimal, clear, action-oriented |
| Landing Pages | Assertive, credible, focused on value |
| Reports | Analytical and neutral |
| System Messages | Short, helpful, non-alarming |
| Documentation | Structured, step-by-step, objective |

---

## Formatting Principles
- Always use **sentence case**.  
- Keep paragraphs short and readable.  
- Prefer **active voice**.  
- Avoid unnecessary adjectives.  
- No emojis or slang in professional content.  

---

## Prohibited Phrases
- “Guaranteed profits”  
- “Risk-free”  
- “Unlimited results”  
- “Effortless success”  

---

## Key Pillars
- Clarity → users understand instantly.  
- Accuracy → content matches reality.  
- Trust → tone feels authoritative and responsible.
