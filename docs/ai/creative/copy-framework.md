# ✍️ Copy-Framework.md — Writing System and Tone Rules

## Purpose
Establish unified standards for text across marketing, UI, and documentation.

---

## Voice Overview
- Professional, confident, and empathetic.  
- Clear > clever.  
- Active > passive.  
- Positive > negative framing.  

---

## Structural Guidelines
### Headlines
- Start with a verb or benefit.  
- 6–10 words max.  
- Avoid filler (“Welcome to”, “Introducing”).  

### Subheadlines
- Clarify what, why, or how in one concise line.  

### Body Copy
- Short paragraphs (2–3 sentences).  
- Use parallel structure for lists.  
- Avoid redundancy and hedging words (“really”, “very”).  

### Buttons / CTAs
- Begin with a verb: *Start*, *Create*, *Explore*, *Book*, *Learn More*.  

---

## Tone Matrix
| Context | Tone | Example |
|----------|------|----------|
| Landing Page | Assertive & optimistic | “Automate your workflow in minutes.” |
| Product UI | Neutral & helpful | “Changes saved successfully.” |
| Error Message | Calm & instructive | “Something went wrong — try again.” |
| Email / Notification | Friendly & direct | “Your report is ready to download.” |

---

## Grammar & Style
- Use contractions (“you’ll”, “we’re”).  
- Write in sentence case.  
- Oxford comma allowed.  
- Numbers 0–9 spelled out in text; 10 + as numerals.  
- Never use exclamation marks in system messages.  

---

## Terminology Rules
- Use *sign in* (not *log in*).  
- Use *dashboard*, not *portal*.  
- Avoid jargon unless targeting technical audiences.
