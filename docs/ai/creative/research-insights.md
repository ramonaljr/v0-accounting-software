# 🔍 Research-Insights.md — Findings and Evidence Log

## Purpose
Record validated insights from user studies, analytics, and market research to inform design and strategy decisions.

---

## Entry Template
Each insight should include:
- **Date**
- **Source / Method**
- **Key Finding**
- **Impact Level** (High | Medium | Low)
- **Recommendation**
- **Status** (Applied | Pending | Archived)

---

## Example
**Date:** 2025-10-10  
**Source:** UX survey (N = 200)  
**Finding:** 70 % of users abandon setup if onboarding > 3 steps.  
**Impact:** High  
**Recommendation:** Reduce initial steps to 2 and add progress bar.  
**Status:** Applied  

---

## Research Categories
- **User Experience:** usability tests, task success rates.  
- **Market:** competitor feature benchmarks.  
- **Performance:** load times, error logs.  
- **Messaging:** A/B test results, copy resonance data.  

---

## Insight Lifecycle
1. Collect → Analyze → Prioritize → Apply → Archive.  
2. Summarize top 3 insights weekly for AI Ops review.  
3. Reference relevant insights in `decision-log.md` when implementing.
